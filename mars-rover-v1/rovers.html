Mission History & Goals

The Mars Rover Program has launched multiple missions to explore the Red Planet. Each rover aimed to study Mars’ geology, climate, and search for evidence of past life.

Sojourner

Launch Date: December 4, 1996

  • Tested rover technology on Mars.
  • Analyzed soil and rocks in Ares Vallis.
  • Provided data for future missions.

Spirit

Launch Date: June 10, 2003

  • Explored Gusev Crater for past water evidence.
  • Analyzed Martian geology and soils.
  • Operated until 2010 providing long-term data.

Opportunity

Launch Date: July 7, 2003

  • Explored Meridiani Planum.
  • Analyzed rocks for past water signs.
  • Sent long-term data for future exploration.

Curiosity

Launch Date: November 26, 2011

  • Investigated Gale Crater.
  • Studied habitability and past water activity.
  • Collected soil and rock data.

Perseverance

Launch Date: July 30, 2020

  • Search for signs of past life.
  • Collect rock samples for return missions.
  • Test technology for human exploration.
