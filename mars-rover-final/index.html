About the Mars Rover Program

The NASA Mars Rover Program is a series of robotic missions designed to explore the surface of Mars. Each rover collects data on the planet's geology, climate, and the potential for past or present life.

From Sojourner to Perseverance, NASA has continuously improved its technology, sending mobile laboratories equipped with advanced instruments to study the Red Planet.

These missions have provided stunning images, soil analyses, and critical data that pave the way for future human exploration of Mars.
